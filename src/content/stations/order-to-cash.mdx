<Level n={1} title="The gist" minutes={3} teaser="The sell-side mirror of procure to pay, step by step in SAP." next="How it works" nextMinutes={4}>

Order to cash in SAP SD mirrors procure to pay from the seller's side. In fact, your customer's purchase order becomes your sales order:

| Step | T-code | What happens | Finance posting |
|---|---|---|---|
| Inquiry, quotation (optional) | VA11, VA21 | Customer asks for price and availability; you send a binding offer | None |
| Sales order | VA01 | Pricing, availability (ATP) and credit checks; requirement passed to MRP | None |
| Outbound delivery | VL01N or VL10 | Shipping document created from the order | None |
| Picking and packing | VL02N (or WM/EWM tasks) | Goods staged for the customer | None |
| Post goods issue | VL02N, movement type 601 | Stock leaves; ownership passes | Dr Cost of goods sold / Cr Inventory |
| Billing | VF01 or VF04 | Customer invoice | Dr Customer / Cr Revenue (and tax) |
| Incoming payment | F-28 | Payment clears the open invoice | Dr Bank / Cr Customer |

Every sales document belongs to a **sales area**: sales organization + distribution channel + division. A customer must be set up for the sales area you sell through, which is a common source of "customer not defined for sales area" errors.

**Pricing** uses the **condition technique**: a pricing procedure lists condition types in sequence, such as base price, discounts, freight and tax, and SAP looks up condition records for each one (maintained with VK11). The choice of condition type matters. A percentage discount calculated on the net price instead of the gross price gives a different invoice amount.

The **document flow** (visible from VA03) links inquiry, quotation, order, delivery, goods issue, invoice and payment, so you can trace any order end to end.

<FromPractice>

In Global Bike I sold to the customer **The Bike Zone 077**, reusing the existing record rather than creating a duplicate; the system already held dozens of "The Bike Zone" copies, which is duplicate master data in miniature. Selling helmets meant the customer had to be extended to another division, because helmets and bikes sit in different divisions. And choosing discount condition **RA00** (percentage from net) instead of **RA01** (percentage from gross) changed my invoice by 12.50.

</FromPractice>

<KeyIdea>

Order to cash: sales order (VA01) → delivery (VL01N) → pick → post goods issue (601, COGS) → billing (VF01, revenue) → payment (F-28). Pricing runs on the condition technique, and the sales area decides who can sell what to whom.

</KeyIdea>

<Level n={2} title="How it works" minutes={4} teaser="The configuration behind a sales order (document types, item and schedule line categories), pricing procedure determination, and a discount calculated two ways." next="Deep dive" nextMinutes={4}>

### What a sales order is made of

- **Sales document type:** such as OR (standard order), QT (quotation), IN (inquiry), RE (returns), CR (credit memo request).
- **Item category:** determined from the document type and the material's item category group, such as TAN (standard), TANN (free of charge) or TAS (third-party). It controls pricing, delivery and billing relevance.
- **Schedule line category:** such as CP, which passes requirements to MRP, or CN, which doesn't. It decides whether an order creates demand for planning.
- **Copy control:** rules for creating one document from another (quotation to order, order to delivery, delivery to invoice).

### Pricing procedure determination

The pricing procedure for an order is found from three keys: the **sales area**, the **document pricing procedure** (from the document type) and the **customer pricing procedure** (from the customer master). This determination is configured in OVKK. Each condition type in the procedure, for example the base price (PR00 in ECC, PPR0 in S/4HANA), material and customer discounts (K004, K007), percentage discounts (RA00, RA01) and freight (KF00), has an **access sequence** that searches condition records from the most specific to the most general.

<Example title="the same 5% discount, from net or from gross">

Gross price 1,000; an absolute discount of 50 earlier in the procedure leaves 950. A 5% discount then gives:

- **RA00 (percentage from net):** 5% of 950 = 47.50.
- **RA01 (percentage from gross):** 5% of 1,000 = 50.00.

The difference is 2.50 a unit, or 12.50 on five units. Choosing the condition type decides which base the percentage applies to, which is how an invoice can change by a small amount that looks like a rounding error but isn't.

</Example>

### Checks at order entry

- **Availability (ATP):** controlled by the material's checking group and the checking rule for the transaction.
- **Credit:** in S/4HANA, SAP Credit Management checks the order against the customer's credit limit and blocks it if needed; blocked orders are released by credit management.
- **Incompleteness:** the incompletion log lists missing data, such as a missing purchase order number or delivery date, that blocks follow-on steps.

### Revenue postings

Billing posts revenue to accounts found by **revenue account determination** (VKOA), using keys such as ERL for revenue and ERS for sales deductions, combined with the customer's and material's account assignment groups.

<KeyIdea>

Document type, item category and schedule line category decide how an order behaves; OVKK finds the pricing procedure, access sequences find condition records, and the choice of condition type (such as RA00 or RA01) changes the result. ATP, credit and incompletion checks guard the order, and VKOA sends revenue to the right accounts.

</KeyIdea>

<Level n={3} title="Deep dive" minutes={4} teaser="Special sales processes, advanced ATP and product allocation, rebates in S/4HANA, the key tables behind document flow, and troubleshooting stuck orders.">

### Special sales processes

- **Consignment:** stock at the customer's site remains yours until used: fill-up (KB), issue (KE), pick-up (KA) and returns (KR).
- **Third-party orders:** item category TAS creates a purchase requisition so the supplier ships directly to the customer.
- **Returns and credit memos:** a returns order (RE) brings goods back; credit memo requests (CR) and debit memo requests (DR) correct invoices.
- **Cash sales (BV) and rush orders (SO):** delivery and billing happen immediately or the same day.
- **Intercompany sales:** one company code sells while another delivers, with internal billing between them.

### Advanced ATP and product allocation

S/4HANA's **advanced ATP** adds:

- **Product allocation:** quotas by customer, region or channel, consumed by orders.
- **Alternative-based confirmation:** checking other plants or substitute products.
- **Backorder processing:** re-confirming open orders by priority when supply changes, with rules such as "win", "gain", "redistribute" and "fill" for different customer groups.

### Rebates in S/4HANA

Classic SD rebate agreements are replaced in S/4HANA by **condition contracts** in settlement management, which handle accruals and settlement of rebates and other incentives.

### The tables behind the flow

Orders are stored in VBAK and VBAP (header and items), deliveries in LIKP and LIPS, billing documents in VBRK and VBRP, and the links between them in VBFA, the document flow. Knowing them helps when building reports or tracing what happened to an order.

### Troubleshooting stuck orders

- **Order won't save or is incomplete:** check the incompletion log.
- **Order blocked:** credit block, delivery block or a missing approval.
- **No delivery created:** no stock confirmed by ATP, a delivery block, a wrong shipping point or route, or the delivery date not yet due.
- **No billing:** billing block, delivery not goods-issued, or the item isn't billing-relevant.
- **Invoice not in accounting:** revenue account determination failed; fix VKOA and release the billing document to accounting (VFX3).

<Trap>

Answering "the invoice didn't post to finance" with "the system has a bug" misses the usual cause: missing account determination for that combination of account key and account assignment groups. Check VKOA before anything else.

</Trap>

<KeyIdea>

Know the special processes (consignment, third-party, returns, cash and rush orders, intercompany), use advanced ATP for allocation and backorder processing, expect condition contracts for rebates in S/4HANA, know the order tables and VBFA, and troubleshoot stuck orders step by step from incompletion to accounting release.

</KeyIdea>

</Level>

<Q q="What happens in the system at post goods issue?">

Stock is reduced in the plant and storage location, ownership passes to the customer, a material document and an accounting document are created (cost of goods sold debited, inventory credited), the delivery status updates and the delivery becomes due for billing.

</Q>

<Q q="How does pricing work in SAP SD?">

Through the condition technique. A pricing procedure, determined from the sales area and document and customer settings, lists condition types such as base price, discounts, surcharges and tax. For each one, an access sequence searches condition tables for the most specific valid condition record, and the procedure calculates the net value step by step.

</Q>

<Q q="What does 'customer not defined for sales area' mean?">

The customer's sales data hasn't been created for the sales organization, distribution channel and division used in the order. The Business Partner must be extended to that sales area before you can sell through it.

</Q>
